Event layout planning

Organize Your Event into Activity Zones

Large corporate events operate more smoothly when entertainment, food, seating and service areas are clearly separated.

  • Place children’s attractions together.
  • Keep competitive activities in an open area.
  • Separate food service from inflatables.
  • Protect generators and electrical cords.
  • Keep emergency and vehicle access clear.
  • Provide visible event signs and information.

Outdoor Corporate Events

Outdoor events require safe ground, equipment access, approved anchoring and weather monitoring.

  • Confirm grass, asphalt or concrete.
  • Identify underground utilities.
  • Confirm water and electrical access.
  • Provide a weather backup plan.

Indoor Corporate Events

Some equipment may be installed indoors when the building meets size, access and power requirements.

  • Confirm ceiling height.

  • Confirm flooring requirements.
  • Verify approved electrical circuits.

Power Planning

Electrical requirements

Reliable Power Keeps the Event Running

Inflatable blowers require continuous power. Large events may need several generators or separate electrical circuits.

  • Confirm outlet locations and circuit capacity.
  • Avoid circuits used by food equipment.
  • Keep generators in approved outdoor areas.
  • Protect cords from vehicle and foot traffic.
  • Reserve backup power when appropriate.

Delivery coordination

Help Our Crew Access the Setup Area

Corporate campuses and private venues may have security desks, loading docks, parking restrictions and limited delivery windows.

  • Provide an on-site contact.
  • Confirm security and check-in procedures.
  • Reserve a loading or parking area.
  • Identify the exact setup location.
  • Tell us about stairs, elevators or long distances.
  • Confirm the required pickup deadline.
