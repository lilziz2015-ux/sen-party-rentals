Participant Guidelines

Participants should be grouped with children of similar age, height and size. The exact number allowed inside depends on the participants’ combined weight and the manufacturer’s safety requirements.

A responsible adult must supervise the bounce house at all times. Shoes, food, drinks, candy, face paint, sharp objects, confetti and silly string are not allowed inside.

Setup Requirements

The setup area must be reasonably level, clean and clear of branches, rocks, sticks, pet waste, irrigation equipment, underground hazards and overhead obstacles.

Additional space is required around the bounce house for stakes, anchoring equipment, the blower and safe participant entry.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, artificial turf or indoors before completing your reservation. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval.

Electrical Requirements

The SpongeBob Bounce House requires a suitable electrical outlet for its blower. The outlet should not share a circuit with high-powered appliances or another inflatable blower.

Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable or too far from the setup area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the SpongeBob Bounce House.

What are the dimensions?

The SpongeBob Bounce House measures approximately 15 feet long, 15 feet wide and 15 feet high. Additional clearance is required around the unit for safe anchoring and operation.

How many children can jump at one time?

Capacity depends on the participants’ ages, sizes and combined weight. Children should be grouped by similar size and supervised by a responsible adult.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the bounce house. Delivery fees may vary depending on the event location.

Does it require electricity?

Yes. One suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when power is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete?

Concrete or asphalt setup may be available with advance notice. Different anchoring equipment and additional setup requirements may apply.

Can adults use this bounce house?

This inflatable is primarily intended for children. All participants must follow the posted height, weight and safety restrictions.

What happens when bad weather is expected?

Setup and use depend on rain, wind and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals before your event when bad weather is expected.
