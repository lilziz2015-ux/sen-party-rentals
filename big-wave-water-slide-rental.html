Water Requirements

The customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a water hose long enough to reach the inflatable setup location.

The water connection should remain available while the slide is operating. The water pressure must be sufficient to keep the slide lane properly wet.

The setup area must have suitable drainage. Water cannot collect near buildings, electrical equipment, parking areas, sidewalks or neighboring properties.

Participant Guidelines

Only one participant should use the slide lane at a time unless the posted manufacturer instructions state otherwise. Each participant must leave the landing area before the next rider begins.

Riders must slide feet-first while seated or lying on their backs. Head-first sliding, standing, flipping, rough play, jumping from the platform and climbing on the outside walls are prohibited.

A responsible adult must supervise the water slide continuously. Shoes, jewelry, glasses, food, drinks, sharp objects, face paint, confetti and silly string are not permitted on the inflatable.

Setup Requirements

The setup location must be reasonably level, clean and free of rocks, sticks, branches, pet waste, sprinkler heads, irrigation equipment, underground hazards and overhead obstructions.

Additional space is required around the inflatable for safety stakes, anchoring equipment, blower placement, water connections and participant access.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt or artificial turf before booking. Hard-surface installations require advance approval and different anchoring equipment.

Electrical Requirements

The Big Wave Water Slide requires one suitable electrical outlet for its commercial blower. The outlet should not share a circuit with major appliances or another inflatable blower.

Generator rentals are available for parks, open fields and other event locations where suitable electricity is unavailable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Big Wave Water Slide.

How big is the Big Wave Water Slide?

It measures approximately 30 feet long, 12 feet wide and 18 feet high. Additional clearance is required around the inflatable for anchoring and safe operation.

Does the customer provide the water?

Yes. The customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a hose long enough to reach the setup location.

Is setup and takedown included?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the water slide. Delivery charges may vary depending on location.

Does the Big Wave require electricity?

Yes. One suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be possible with advance approval. Different anchoring, padding and safety requirements may apply.

Can adults use the water slide?

Use depends on the manufacturer’s height and weight limits. All riders must follow the posted operating and safety rules.

How many riders can slide at one time?

Only one rider should use the slide lane at a time unless the posted manufacturer instructions specifically allow otherwise.

What happens if bad weather is expected?

Setup and operation depend on wind, rain, lightning and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals at 571-719-9575 when severe weather is expected.
