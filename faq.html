Planning your rental

These are some of the most important things to know before your delivery date.

Measure the setup area

Make sure there is enough level space for the rental, blower, anchors, entrances and safe clearance.

Confirm power access

Most inflatables require a dedicated electrical outlet or an appropriately sized generator.

Watch the weather

Wind, lightning and severe rain may require the equipment to be evacuated or shut down.
