Measure before booking

Make Sure the Rental Fits Your Space

Every inflatable needs enough room for the unit, blower, anchoring, entrances and safe clearance.

  • Measure the available length and width.
  • Check for trees, fences and overhead wires.
  • Remove furniture, toys and yard equipment.
  • Confirm the ground is level and clear.
  • Keep gates and delivery paths accessible.
  • Tell us about stairs, hills or narrow entrances.

Electrical Power

Inflatable blowers require continuous electricity from an approved outlet or generator.

  • Outlet should be close to the setup area.
  • Avoid overloaded circuits.
  • Generator rentals are available.
View Generators

Water Access

Water slides and dunk tanks need a working outdoor water source nearby.

  • Confirm the faucet works.
  • Make sure enough hose is available.
  • Keep the setup area properly drained.

Safe birthday fun

Keep Every Guest Safe

  • Group children by similar age and size.
  • Follow the posted capacity limits.
  • Remove shoes, food, drinks and sharp objects.
  • Do not allow flips, wrestling or rough play.
  • Keep entrances and exits clear.
  • Stop use during unsafe weather.
