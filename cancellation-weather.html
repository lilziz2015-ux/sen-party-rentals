Safety comes first

Unsafe Weather May Require Cancellation

Sen Party Rentals may delay, modify, reschedule or cancel a rental when weather or site conditions create a safety risk.

Final decisions may depend on actual conditions at the event location, not only the forecast.

Weather conditions

When Rentals Must Stop

Inflatables and outdoor rental equipment should not be used when conditions become unsafe.

Strong Wind

Inflatables must not operate during strong or gusty winds that could affect stability.

Lightning

Equipment use must stop immediately when thunder or lightning is present.

Heavy Rain

Heavy rain, flooding and poor visibility can make rental equipment unsafe.

Snow and Ice

Snow, ice and frozen ground may prevent safe delivery, anchoring or operation.

Extreme Heat

Inflatable surfaces may become dangerously hot during extreme temperatures and direct sunlight.

Unsafe Site Conditions

Mud, standing water, soft ground or inaccessible property may prevent setup.

Rain policy

What Happens if Rain Is Forecast?

A chance of rain does not always mean your rental will automatically be canceled. Forecasts can change, and light rain may not affect every rental in the same way.

We may review the forecast, wind, storm timing, setup surface and event location before making a final decision.

  • Light rain may not require cancellation.
  • Heavy rain may prevent safe operation.
  • Thunder or lightning requires immediate shutdown.
  • Standing water may prevent setup or pickup.
  • Wet equipment must not be used when unsafe.

Forecasts Can Change

Weather decisions may be made closer to the event when the forecast is uncertain.

Customers should remain available by phone, text or email for updates.

Wind safety

Wind May Require Immediate Shutdown

Inflatables can become dangerous when wind or wind gusts exceed safe operating conditions. Even properly anchored equipment may not be used during unsafe wind.

  • Follow all instructions provided at setup.
  • Watch for sudden gusts and changing conditions.
  • Remove all participants when wind increases.
  • Do not continue use because the event is still active.
  • Contact Sen Party Rentals when guidance is needed.
Customer-requested cancellations

Canceling Your Reservation

Contact us as soon as possible when your plans change.

Advance Cancellation

Cancellations submitted before the delivery date may be eligible for rescheduling or rental credit, depending on the booking agreement.

Late Cancellation

Late cancellations may result in the loss of deposits, payments or delivery charges.

After Dispatch

Delivery and labor charges may remain payable after our vehicle or crew has been dispatched.

No Access or No Show

Payments may be forfeited when delivery cannot be completed because no one is available or the property cannot be accessed.

Moving your event

Rescheduling Your Rental

Rescheduling may be available when the new date is requested in advance and the rental equipment is available.

  • The new date is subject to availability.
  • Rental prices may change for the new date.
  • Delivery fees may change for a new location.
  • Credits may have an expiration date.
  • Credits may apply only to the original customer.
  • Holiday and peak-season restrictions may apply.

Request a New Date

Contact us with your original event date and the new date you are considering.

Payments and credits

Deposits, Balances and Refunds

Deposits

A deposit may be required to reserve equipment. Deposits may become nonrefundable after the reservation is confirmed.

Remaining Balance

The remaining balance must be paid according to the rental confirmation or written agreement.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on timing, the cancellation reason, completed work and the terms of the confirmed reservation.

Rental Credit

In some cases, approved payments may be transferred to a future available rental date.

Your booking confirmation, invoice or rental agreement may contain specific terms that apply to your reservation. Those written terms control when they differ from this general policy page.

When We May Cancel

  • Unsafe weather conditions.
  • Dangerous wind or lightning.
  • Flooding or unstable ground.
  • Unsafe access or setup location.
  • Equipment damage or unexpected mechanical problems.
  • Conditions that violate safety requirements.
Sen Party Rentals decisions

Safety Decisions Are Final

Sen Party Rentals may refuse or stop a setup when our team determines that delivery, installation or equipment use would be unsafe.

We will make reasonable efforts to offer an available replacement, rescheduled date, rental credit or another appropriate resolution.

The available option will depend on timing, equipment availability and the circumstances surrounding the cancellation.
