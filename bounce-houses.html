Before delivery

Bounce House Setup Requirements

Preparing a safe and accessible setup area helps our team complete your installation properly.

Clear Setup Space

The setup area must be larger than the inflatable and clear of fences, branches and overhead obstacles.

Approved Surface

Grass is preferred. Concrete and asphalt setups must be approved before your delivery date.

Electrical Power

A working electrical outlet should be available within a safe distance of the inflatable.

Delivery Access

Gates, walkways and entry points must be wide enough for the inflatable and delivery equipment.
