Safe summer fun

Water Slide Safety Rules

Adult supervision and proper use are required during the entire rental period.

Adult Supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the inflatable at all times while it is being used.

No Rough Play

Flips, pushing, wrestling, climbing on walls and unsafe behavior are not permitted.

Similar-Sized Riders

Riders should be grouped by similar age and size to reduce the risk of collisions.

Stop During Bad Weather

Do not use the inflatable during lightning, thunderstorms, heavy rain or high winds.

Prepare your setup area

Water Slide Setup Requirements

Please review these requirements before requesting your water slide rental.

Water Access

A working outdoor water faucet must be available within reach of the setup area.

Electrical Outlet

A working electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when needed.

Clear Setup Space

The setup area must be larger than the inflatable and clear of trees, wires, fences and sharp objects.

Surface Type

Grass is preferred. Concrete, asphalt or indoor setups must be approved before delivery.

Delivery Access

Gates and walkways must be wide enough for the inflatable, equipment and delivery cart.

Water Drainage

The customer is responsible for confirming that water can safely drain from the setup area.
