Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Important details about delivery, setup, booking and payment.

What areas do you deliver to?
We deliver throughout Manassas, Prince William County, Fairfax County, Loudoun County, Stafford, Fredericksburg, Washington, D.C. and other nearby DMV communities. Delivery availability and pricing depend on the event location.
Do you provide setup and pickup?
Yes. We handle delivery, professional setup, equipment securing and pickup after the event.
How early should I reserve?
Reserving at least two weeks early is recommended. Popular summer weekends, holiday weekends and large-event dates may require earlier booking.
What payment methods do you accept?
No deposit is required; pay after setup. We accept Zelle, cash, check and cards. A processing fee applies only to card payments and is disclosed before payment. There is no processing fee for cash or check.
Can inflatables be set up on concrete?
Some equipment can be installed on concrete or asphalt using approved anchoring methods such as sandbags. Tell us the surface type when requesting your booking.
What happens if bad weather is expected?
Weather conditions are reviewed before delivery. Inflatables cannot safely operate during strong winds, severe storms or other unsafe conditions.
