How Many Generators Do You Need?

The correct number depends on the wattage of the generator and the starting and running power required by each blower or machine. Larger rental packages may need more than one generator.

Send us your complete equipment list before the event. We will help determine whether one generator is sufficient or whether separate power sources are required.

Important: Do not connect additional equipment to the generator without approval. Overloading the generator can shut down the blower and create an unsafe condition for inflatable participants.

Generator Safety Requirements

Fuel-powered generators must be operated outdoors in a well-ventilated area. They must never be placed inside a home, garage, tent, enclosed trailer or other enclosed structure.

The generator must remain away from doors, windows, air intakes, guest seating, food service areas and heavy pedestrian traffic. Children and unauthorized guests should not touch or operate it.

The generator must remain dry and should not be moved, refueled or adjusted while it is operating. Follow all instructions provided by Sen Party Rentals.

Fuel and Runtime

Fuel and runtime requirements depend on the generator, event duration and connected equipment. Confirm the included fuel amount and expected operating time when reserving.

Longer events may require additional fuel arrangements. Never refuel a hot or running generator.

Placement Requirements

The generator requires a stable, level outdoor surface with enough clearance for airflow and safe operation. It should be placed where the extension cord can reach the equipment without crossing busy walkways.

Final placement is subject to safety conditions at the event location.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information before adding a generator to your rental.

How much is a generator rental?

Generator rentals start at $100 each. Delivery, fuel and extended runtime may affect the final price.

Do I need a generator for a bounce house?

You may need one when a suitable outlet is unavailable, too far from the setup area or already being used by other high-power equipment.

How many inflatables can one generator power?

It depends on the generator output and the blower requirements. We will review your complete rental package and confirm the correct setup.

Is fuel included?

Fuel details depend on the event length and rental agreement. Confirm the included fuel and expected runtime when booking.

Can I use the generator indoors?

No. Fuel-powered generators must only operate outdoors in a well-ventilated area away from doors, windows and occupied spaces.

Can I connect my own equipment?

Only approved equipment should be connected. Additional devices may overload the generator and interrupt power to the inflatable.

Can I move the generator after setup?

No. Contact Sen Party Rentals before moving or adjusting the generator because safe placement and cord routing are important.

Do you deliver generators without an inflatable rental?

Availability may depend on the event date, location and delivery schedule. Contact us for a generator-only rental quote.
