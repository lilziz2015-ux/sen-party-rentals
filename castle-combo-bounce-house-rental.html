About This Inflatable

More Than Just a Bounce House

The Castle Combo is one of our most popular inflatable rentals because it offers multiple activities inside one unit. Instead of only bouncing, children can jump, climb and slide throughout the inflatable, keeping everyone entertained longer.

The colorful castle theme works perfectly for birthday parties, school events, church festivals, company picnics, daycare events, neighborhood gatherings and community celebrations throughout Northern Virginia.

During warmer months the Castle Combo can also be rented with water, turning the slide into an exciting splash experience.

Setup Requirements

Space Required

Allow approximately 35 feet long, 18 feet wide and 18 feet high for safe installation.

Power

One dedicated household outlet within 75 feet of the setup area.

Surface

Grass is preferred. Concrete and asphalt require advance approval.

Wet Rental

Customer supplies a water hose reaching the inflatable.


Safety Guidelines

Children should be grouped by similar age and size.

Shoes, food, drinks, gum, face paint, silly string, sharp objects and pets are not permitted inside.

Adult supervision is required during the entire rental.
