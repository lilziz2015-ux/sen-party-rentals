Our story

Helping Local Events Come to Life

Sen Party Rentals is a local party equipment rental company based in Manassas, Virginia.

We provide inflatables and event equipment for birthdays, graduations, family reunions, church events, school functions, festivals, fundraisers, company gatherings and neighborhood celebrations.

Our goal is to make renting party equipment simple. We focus on clear communication, dependable delivery, clean equipment and professional setup.
