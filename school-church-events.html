Large-event planning

Build Multiple Activity Zones

Larger events work best when activities are separated by participant age, equipment type and expected traffic.

  • Create a toddler or younger-child area.
  • Place high-capacity attractions in open areas.
  • Keep food and drinks away from inflatables.
  • Provide separate entry and exit paths.
  • Assign trained adult supervisors.
  • Keep generators and cords away from guests.

Reliable Power Planning

Electricity and generators

Every Inflatable Needs Continuous Power

Before your event, confirm that approved electrical outlets are available close to the setup area.

Multiple inflatables may require separate circuits or generators to prevent power interruptions.

  • Confirm outlet locations.
  • Identify the available circuits.
  • Avoid sharing circuits with kitchen equipment.
  • Keep cords protected from guests.
  • Reserve generators when power is uncertain.

Setup options

Indoor and Outdoor Events

Outdoor Setup

Outdoor installations require a clear, level setup area and safe anchoring conditions.

  • Grass is usually preferred.
  • Underground utilities must be identified.
  • Weather must be monitored.
  • Vehicle access must be available.

Indoor Setup

Some inflatables may fit inside gymnasiums, fellowship halls or event centers.

  • Confirm ceiling height.
  • Measure doors and hallways.
  • Protect approved flooring.
  • Confirm electrical access.
Delivery coordination

Plan Access Before Event Day

Schools and churches may have locked gates, loading zones, security procedures and limited delivery windows.

  • Provide the on-site contact’s phone number.
  • Confirm building or gate access.
  • Reserve a loading area when required.
  • Identify the exact setup location.
  • Tell us about stairs, elevators or long walks.
  • Confirm when all equipment must be removed.
View Delivery Information

Assign Adult Supervisors

Each attraction should have a responsible adult actively controlling entry, capacity and participant behavior.

Event safety

Supervision Is Required at All Times

  • Group participants by similar age and size.
  • Follow posted equipment capacities.
  • Keep entrances and exits clear.
  • Do not allow rough play or flips.
  • Stop use during unsafe weather.
  • Keep food, drinks and shoes outside.
