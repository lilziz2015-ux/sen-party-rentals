Participant Guidelines

Participants should be grouped by similar age, height and size. The number of children allowed inside depends on their combined weight and the inflatable’s safety limits.

A responsible adult must supervise the High Sky Combo whenever it is in use. Shoes, food, drinks, candy, face paint, sharp objects, confetti and silly string are not allowed inside.

Setup Requirements

The setup location must be level, clean and free of branches, rocks, sticks, pet waste, irrigation equipment, underground hazards and overhead obstacles.

Additional clearance is required around the inflatable for stakes, anchoring equipment, the blower and safe participant access.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, artificial turf or indoors before booking. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval.

Electrical Requirements

The High Sky Combo requires a suitable electrical outlet for the blower. The outlet should not share a circuit with major appliances or other inflatable blowers.

Generator rentals are available for parks, fields and locations where electrical power is unavailable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the High Sky Combo.

What are the dimensions of the High Sky Combo?

The High Sky Combo measures approximately 32 feet long, 13 feet wide and 16 feet high. Additional space is required around the inflatable for safe setup and operation.

What activities does it include?

The inflatable includes a large bounce area, an interior climbing section and an inflatable slide.

How many children can use it at one time?

Capacity depends on the ages, sizes and combined weight of the participants. Children should be grouped by similar size and supervised by an adult.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the High Sky Combo. Delivery fees may vary by location.

Does it require electricity?

Yes. A suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be possible with advance approval. Different anchoring equipment and additional requirements may apply.

Can adults use the High Sky Combo?

The inflatable is primarily intended for children. All users must follow the posted height, weight and safety restrictions.

What happens if bad weather is expected?

Setup and operation depend on rain, wind and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals at 571-719-9575 when bad weather is expected.
