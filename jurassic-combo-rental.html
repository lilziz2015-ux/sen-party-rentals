Participant Guidelines

Children should be grouped with participants of similar age, height and size. The number of children allowed inside depends on their combined weight and the inflatable’s safety limits.

A responsible adult must supervise the Jurassic Combo whenever it is in use. Shoes, food, drinks, candy, face paint, sharp objects, confetti and silly string are not allowed inside.

Setup Requirements

The setup area must be level, clean and clear of rocks, sticks, branches, pet waste, underground utilities, irrigation equipment and overhead obstructions.

Additional clearance is required around the inflatable for stakes, anchoring equipment, the blower and safe participant entry and exit.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, artificial turf or indoors before booking. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval and different anchoring equipment.

Electrical Requirements

The Jurassic Combo requires one suitable electrical outlet for the blower. The outlet should not share its circuit with major appliances or another inflatable blower.

Generator rentals are available for parks, fields and other locations where electrical power is unavailable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Jurassic Combo.

How big is the Jurassic Combo?

The Jurassic Combo measures approximately 28 feet long, 13 feet wide and 16 feet high. Additional space is required around the inflatable for safe setup and operation.

Does the Jurassic Combo include a slide?

Yes. It includes a spacious bounce area, an interior climbing wall and an inflatable slide.

How many children can use it at one time?

Capacity depends on the ages, sizes and combined weight of the participants. Children should be grouped by similar size and supervised by a responsible adult.

Is setup included?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the Jurassic Combo. Delivery fees may vary depending on location.

Does it require electricity?

Yes. One suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be possible with advance approval. Different anchoring equipment and additional setup requirements may apply.

Can adults use the Jurassic Combo?

The inflatable is designed primarily for children. All participants must follow the posted height, weight and safety requirements.

What happens if bad weather is expected?

Setup and operation depend on rain, wind and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals at 571-719-9575 when bad weather is expected.
