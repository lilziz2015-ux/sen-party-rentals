Wet Rental Requirements

For wet use, the customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a hose long enough to reach the inflatable setup location.

The water connection should remain available while the slide is operating. Water pressure must be sufficient to keep the slide lane properly wet.

The setup area must also provide suitable drainage so water does not collect near buildings, electrical equipment, parking areas, sidewalks or neighboring properties.

Dry Rental Option

The Tropical Thunder can also be reserved without water. Dry use is a good option for cooler weather, indoor-approved locations or events where a water connection is unavailable.

Tell Sen Party Rentals whether you need wet or dry use when requesting your reservation.

Participant Guidelines

Only one rider should use the slide lane at a time unless the posted operating instructions state otherwise. The previous rider must exit the landing area before the next rider begins.

Riders must slide feet-first while seated or lying on their backs. Head-first sliding, standing, flipping, jumping from the platform and climbing on exterior walls are prohibited.

A responsible adult must supervise the inflatable continuously. Shoes, jewelry, glasses, food, drinks, sharp objects, face paint, confetti and silly string are not allowed.

Setup Requirements

The setup location must be reasonably level, clean and free of rocks, branches, sticks, pet waste, underground hazards, irrigation equipment and overhead obstructions.

Additional clearance is required around the inflatable for stakes, anchoring equipment, the blower, water connections and safe participant entry and exit.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, artificial turf or indoors before booking. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval.

Electrical Requirements

The Tropical Thunder requires a suitable electrical outlet for its commercial blower. The outlet should not share a circuit with major appliances or another inflatable blower.

Generator rentals are available for parks, open fields and locations where suitable electricity is unavailable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Tropical Thunder Water Slide.

What are the dimensions of the Tropical Thunder?

It measures approximately 28 feet long, 13 feet wide and 16 feet high. Additional space is required around the unit for safe anchoring and operation.

Can the Tropical Thunder be used wet or dry?

Yes. It may be reserved for wet or dry use. Tell us which option you need when requesting your reservation.

Does the customer provide the water hose?

Yes. For wet use, the customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a hose long enough to reach the setup area.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the inflatable. Delivery fees may vary depending on location.

Does the slide require electricity?

Yes. A suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be possible with advance approval. Different anchoring, padding and safety requirements may apply.

How many riders can slide at once?

Only one rider should use the slide lane at a time unless the posted manufacturer instructions specifically allow otherwise.

What happens when bad weather is expected?

Setup and operation depend on wind, rain, lightning and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals at 571-719-9575 when severe weather is expected.
