Prepare the event location

Tent Setup Requirements

Accurate setup information helps us determine whether the selected tent can be installed safely.

Clear Setup Space

The setup area must be larger than the tent and clear of furniture, vehicles and obstacles.

Overhead Clearance

The area must be clear of branches, power lines, roofs and other overhead hazards.

Surface Information

Tell us whether the surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, gravel or another material.

Utility Marking

Underground utilities, irrigation lines and private wiring must be identified before setup.

Delivery Access

The delivery route must allow our crew to safely move tent equipment to the setup area.

Weather Conditions

Tent installation and use may be restricted during high winds, storms or unsafe weather.
