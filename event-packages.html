Package pricing

What affects the final price?

Every event is different. Final package pricing depends on the equipment selected and the work required to deliver and install everything safely.

  • Number and type of rental items
  • Event address and delivery distance
  • Rental duration and event hours
  • Setup surface and anchoring requirements
  • Stairs, hills, gates or difficult access
  • Generator and electrical requirements
  • Holiday and peak-season availability

Package savings

Package discounts may be available when several rental items are delivered and set up at one event.
