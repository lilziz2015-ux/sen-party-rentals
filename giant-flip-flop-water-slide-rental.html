Water connection

Water Requirements

Outdoor Water Source

A working outdoor water connection must be available at the property or event location.

Hose Must Reach

The customer provides a water hose long enough to reach from the connection to the setup area.

Adequate Water Pressure

The water source must provide enough pressure to keep the slide lane wet during normal operation.

Suitable Drainage

The setup area must allow water to drain without flooding buildings, walkways, neighboring properties or electrical equipment.

Water usage reminder

The customer is responsible for water usage, hose access and drainage conditions at the event location.

Blower power

Electrical Requirements

One Dedicated Outlet

The blower requires one suitable household electrical outlet near the setup location.

Outlet Distance

The outlet should normally be within approximately 75 feet of the inflatable setup area.

Avoid Overloaded Circuits

The blower should not share a circuit with major appliances, concession equipment or another inflatable blower.

Generator Available

Generator rentals are available for parks, fields and locations without reliable electrical access.

Space and surface

Setup Requirements

The inflatable itself measures approximately 28 feet long, 12 feet wide and 18 feet high. Additional clearance is required around every side.

Recommended clear setup space Approximately 34′ L × 18′ W × 22′ H

Final space requirements may vary depending on the surface, anchoring method, access route and surrounding obstacles.

Grass Preferred

A clean, reasonably level grass area is generally preferred for safe anchoring and water drainage.

Hard Surface Approval

Concrete, asphalt and artificial turf setups require advance approval and may need special anchoring or padding.

Overhead Clearance

The setup location must be free from tree branches, power lines, lights, roofs and other overhead obstructions.

Delivery Access

Gates, stairs, slopes and narrow walkways must be disclosed before delivery so access can be confirmed.

Clear the Setup Area Before Arrival

  • Remove toys, furniture, grills and yard equipment
  • Pick up rocks, branches, sticks and sharp objects
  • Remove pet waste from the setup and access areas
  • Identify underground irrigation or utility hazards
  • Turn off sprinklers before setup and during the rental
  • Keep vehicles away from the setup and anchoring area
Safe operation

Participant and Supervision Guidelines

One Rider at a Time

Only one participant should use the slide lane at a time unless the posted instructions specifically allow otherwise.

Feet-First Sliding

Riders must slide feet-first while seated or lying on their backs. Head-first sliding is prohibited.

Adult Supervision

A responsible adult must actively supervise the inflatable during the entire period of use.

Clear the Landing

Each rider must leave the splash landing area before the next participant begins climbing or sliding.

Stop During Unsafe Weather

Use must stop during lightning, strong winds, heavy rain or any condition that makes operation unsafe.

Prohibited Items

Shoes, jewelry, glasses, food, gum, sharp objects, face paint, confetti and silly string are not allowed.

Important safety rule

Standing, diving, flipping, pushing, wrestling, climbing on exterior walls and jumping from the top platform are strictly prohibited.

Weather conditions

Rain, Wind and Storm Safety

Water slides are designed for wet operation, but thunderstorms, lightning, strong wind, heavy rain and flooded ground can still create unsafe conditions.

If unsafe weather develops during the event, participants must exit the inflatable and the unit should not be used again until conditions are safe.

Weather-related delivery, setup or cancellation decisions are based on the actual event conditions and the safety of customers, participants and delivery staff.
