Electrical Requirements

The popcorn machine requires a suitable standard electrical outlet. The outlet should be close enough to the serving area for safe cord placement.

Avoid using the same circuit for multiple high-power machines. A generator may be needed at parks, fields or outdoor venues without dependable electrical access.

Setup and Placement Requirements

Place the popcorn machine on a strong, stable and level table or concession stand. The machine should remain under a tent, canopy or covered area when used outdoors.

Keep the machine away from rain, sprinklers, pools, water slides and other sources of moisture. The serving area should also be away from heavy pedestrian traffic and active play zones.

Important: The kettle and interior surfaces become hot during operation. Only a responsible adult should operate the machine, and children should remain away from hot surfaces and electrical cords.

Supplies and Servings

Popcorn kernels, oil, seasoning, serving bags and other supplies may be available separately depending on your rental package. Confirm the number of expected servings when requesting a quote.

For larger events, order enough supplies for the expected guest count and allow time between batches.

Cleaning Responsibilities

Remove remaining popcorn and loose serving materials after use. Do not pour water into the machine or attempt to wash electrical components.

Follow the cleaning and return instructions provided by Sen Party Rentals.

What is included with the rental?

The rental includes the popcorn machine and basic operating instructions. Supplies may be added separately depending on the package.

Does the machine require electricity?

Yes. A suitable standard electrical outlet is required. A generator may be needed for outdoor locations without power.

Yes, but it must remain on a stable surface in a dry, covered location protected from rain, wind and moisture.

A responsible adult should operate and supervise the machine throughout the event.

No. The kettle and interior surfaces become hot. Adult operation and supervision are required.
