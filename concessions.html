Setup and Operating Requirements

Working Power Outlet

The machine must be connected to a safe, working outlet near the serving area.

Stable Table or Stand

Place the machine on a strong, level surface away from the edge.

Adult Operator

A responsible adult should operate and supervise the machine.

Keep Equipment Dry

Protect electrical components from rain, water and wet surfaces.

Hot Surfaces

The popcorn machine may become hot. Children should not operate or touch it.

Basic Cleanup

Remove food, syrup, ice and trash before the machine is returned.
