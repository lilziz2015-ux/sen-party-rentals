Park approval

Permits and Facility Requirements

Each park system may have different rules. Some require a special-use permit, insurance certificate, equipment details or vendor approval.

The customer is responsible for confirming the park’s requirements before delivery.

  • Reserve the picnic shelter or event area.
  • Confirm commercial vendors are allowed.
  • Ask whether inflatables need a separate permit.
  • Confirm staking or anchoring restrictions.
  • Ask whether proof of insurance is required.
  • Confirm arrival and departure time limits.

Electrical access

Many Parks Require a Generator

Inflatable blowers require continuous power. A park outlet may be unavailable, locked, too far from the setup area or not approved for commercial equipment.

  • Confirm whether outlets are available.
  • Ask whether an outlet requires a key.
  • Determine the distance to the setup area.
  • Confirm the circuit can support the equipment.

Water access

Water Slides Need a Working Faucet

Water-slide and dunk-tank rentals require approved water access near the setup area.

  • Confirm the water source is available.
  • Ask whether the faucet requires a key.
  • Bring or request enough hose length.
  • Confirm the park allows water attractions.

Delivery Access
Vehicle and equipment access

Can Our Delivery Vehicle Reach the Area?

Inflatables are large and heavy. Our crew must have reasonable access from the delivery vehicle to the setup location.

  • Confirm vendor vehicle access.
  • Identify the nearest loading area.
  • Ask whether gates are locked.
  • Provide parking instructions.
  • Inform us about hills, stairs or long distances.
  • Confirm the setup area is clearly marked.

Extra labor charges may apply when equipment must be moved a long distance from the delivery vehicle.

Setup surface

Confirm the Ground Type

The setup surface affects anchoring, equipment approval and pricing.

Preferred

Grass

Grass usually allows inflatables to be safely secured with approved stakes.

Asphalt

Asphalt requires advance approval and special weighted anchoring.

Concrete

Concrete setups may require sandbags, extra labor and additional fees.

Athletic Fields

Field use may require separate approval and restrictions on stakes or vehicle access.

Insurance documents

Does the Park Require a Certificate of Insurance?

Some public facilities require vendors to provide proof of insurance or list the city, county or park authority as an additional insured.

Send the exact insurance requirements early. Additional processing time or fees may apply for special documentation.

Supervision and safety

Park Rentals Require Active Adult Supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the equipment continuously and enforce all safety rules.

  • Keep participants grouped by similar size.
  • Follow all equipment capacity limits.
  • Keep guests away from blowers and generators.
  • Stop use during strong wind or lightning.
  • Do not move equipment after setup.
  • Keep the delivery and pickup path clear.
