Participant Guidelines

Children should be grouped with participants of similar age, height and size. The exact number allowed inside depends on the participants’ combined weight and the manufacturer’s safety requirements.

A responsible adult must supervise the bounce house at all times. Shoes, food, drinks, candy, face paint, sharp objects and silly string are not allowed inside the inflatable.

Setup Requirements

The setup location must be reasonably level, clean and clear of rocks, sticks, branches, pet waste, irrigation equipment and other objects that could damage the inflatable.

Please provide additional clearance around the bounce house for stakes, anchoring equipment, the blower and safe participant entry.

Important: Tell us before booking whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt, artificial turf or indoors. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval and different anchoring equipment.

Electrical Requirements

The Princess Castle Bounce House requires a suitable electrical outlet for the blower. The outlet should not share its circuit with high-powered appliances or other inflatable blowers.

Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable or too far from the setup location.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Princess Castle.

What are the dimensions?

The Princess Castle Bounce House measures approximately 13 feet long, 13 feet wide and 16 feet high. Additional space is required around the unit for stakes, the blower and safe entry.

How many children can jump at one time?

Capacity depends on the ages, heights, sizes and combined weight of the children. Participants should be grouped with children of similar size.

Is this bounce house only for girls?

No. Any child who enjoys castles, colorful inflatables and imaginative play can use the Princess Castle Bounce House.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the bounce house. Delivery fees may vary by location.

Does the bounce house require electricity?

Yes. One suitable electrical outlet is required to operate the blower. A generator can be rented when an outlet is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be available with advance notice. Different anchoring equipment and additional setup requirements may apply.

Can the bounce house be used in the rain?

Weather decisions depend on rain, wind and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals before the event when bad weather is expected.
