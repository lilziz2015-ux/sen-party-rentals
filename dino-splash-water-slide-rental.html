Setup Requirements

The Dino Splash measures approximately 25 feet long, 10 feet wide and 16 feet high. Additional open space is required around the inflatable for safe access, anchoring and blower placement.

The setup surface must be level, clean and free from rocks, branches, pet waste, sharp objects, overhead wires and low tree limbs.

Electrical and Water Requirements

A dedicated standard electrical outlet is required within a safe distance of the setup area. The outlet should not be shared with other high-power equipment.

The customer must also provide a working outdoor water connection and a hose long enough to reach the inflatable. Generator rentals are available when suitable electrical power is not available.

Important: Adult supervision is required during the entire rental. The inflatable may not be operated during unsafe wind, lightning, heavy rain or other dangerous weather conditions.

Hard-Surface Setup

Concrete or asphalt setup may be possible with approved commercial sand bag anchoring. Hard-surface installation must be requested in advance and is subject to the inflatable manufacturer’s requirements, available space and weather conditions.
