Plan your setup

Obstacle Course Setup Requirements

These units require more room, power and delivery access than a standard bounce house.

Large Clear Space

The setup area must be longer and wider than the unit, with additional room for blowers and anchor points.

Adequate Power

Large obstacle courses may require two separate electrical circuits or one or more generators.

Setup Surface

Grass is preferred. Concrete, asphalt or indoor installations require advance approval.

Delivery Access

The path from the truck to the setup area must be clear, wide and free of stairs whenever possible.

Overhead Clearance

The area must be clear of tree branches, utility lines, roofs and other overhead hazards.

Adult Supervision

Responsible adults must monitor participants and control how many riders enter the course.

Safe competition

Obstacle Course Safety Rules

01

Similar-Sized Participants

Riders should compete with participants of similar age and size whenever possible.

02

One-Way Traffic

Participants must enter at the designated entrance and exit only through the finishing area.

03

No Rough Play

Pushing, wrestling, flips, climbing on walls and intentionally blocking other riders are prohibited.

04

Stop During Unsafe Weather

The inflatable must not be used during lightning, high winds, severe rain or unsafe conditions.
