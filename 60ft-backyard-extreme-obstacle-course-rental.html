Setup Requirements

The setup area must be reasonably level, clean and free of branches, rocks, pet waste, underground utilities and overhead obstacles. Please allow additional clearance around the full inflatable for anchoring and safe operation.

Important: Please tell us before booking if the setup will be on concrete, asphalt or another hard surface. Different anchoring equipment may be required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving this obstacle course.

How much space is required?

The unit measures approximately 60′ L × 12′ W × 15′ H. Additional space is needed around the inflatable for anchoring, blowers and safe participant entry and exit.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, sets up, secures and later removes the inflatable. Delivery cost depends on your event location.

Do I need electricity?

Yes. Suitable electrical outlets must be available within the approved cord distance. Generator rentals are available when power is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete?

Hard-surface setups may be possible, but they require advance notice and different anchoring methods. Please provide the setup surface when requesting your quote.

What happens if rain is expected?

Contact Sen Party Rentals before the event. Setup decisions may depend on rain, wind and other safety conditions.
