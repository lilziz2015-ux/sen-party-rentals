Event power requirements

When Do You Need a Generator?

A generator may be required when electrical power is unavailable, too far away or unable to safely support your rental equipment.

Park Events

Many public parks do not provide electrical outlets near picnic shelters or inflatable setup areas.

No Nearby Outlet

A generator may be needed when the nearest outlet is beyond the approved extension-cord distance.

Limited Circuits

Multiple inflatables may require separate electrical circuits to prevent overloaded breakers.

Large Events

Schools, churches, festivals and company events may need several generators for multiple rentals.

Safe generator operation

Generator Safety Rules

Outdoor Use Only

Generators must never operate inside homes, garages, tents or enclosed structures.

Keep Equipment Dry

Protect generators, plugs, cords and electrical equipment from water and rain.

Restrict Access

Keep children, guests and pets away from the generator and electrical cords.

Safe Refueling

Turn the generator off and allow it to cool before adding fuel.

Approved Connections

Only approved cords, plugs and outlets should be connected to rental equipment.

Report Problems

Contact Sen Party Rentals immediately if the generator stops or operates abnormally.

Carbon Monoxide Warning

Never operate a generator indoors or near doors, windows, vents or occupied enclosed spaces. Generator exhaust contains carbon monoxide, which can cause serious injury or death.
