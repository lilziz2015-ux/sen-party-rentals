Participant Guidelines

Participants should be grouped by similar age and size. The total number allowed inside depends on the children’s sizes, combined weight and manufacturer safety requirements. A responsible adult must supervise the moon bounce while it is in use.

Setup Requirements

The setup area should be level, clean and free of rocks, branches, pet waste, irrigation equipment, underground utility hazards and overhead obstacles. Additional space is required around the moon bounce for stakes, blower equipment and safe entry.

Important: Please tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt or indoors before booking. Hard-surface and indoor setups require advance approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Modular Moon Bounce.

How many children can jump at one time?

Capacity depends on the participants’ ages, sizes and combined weight. Children should be grouped with others of similar size, and the unit must remain under adult supervision.

What are the dimensions?

The moon bounce measures approximately 13 feet long, 13 feet wide and 15 feet high. Additional clearance is required around the unit for safe setup and anchoring.

Does the rental include setup and takedown?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the moon bounce. Delivery charges depend on the event location.

Does it require electricity?

Yes. A suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is not available near the setup area.

Can adults use this moon bounce?

This unit is primarily intended for children. Participant use must follow the inflatable’s posted weight, height and safety restrictions.

Can it be set up on concrete?

Concrete or asphalt setup may be possible with advance notice. Different anchoring equipment and setup requirements may apply.
