Water Requirements

The customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a water hose long enough to reach the inflatable setup location.

The water connection should remain available while the slide is operating. The water pressure must be sufficient to keep both slide lanes properly wet.

The setup area must have suitable drainage. Water cannot collect near buildings, electrical equipment, sidewalks, parking areas or neighboring properties.

Participant Guidelines

One rider may use each lane at a time. Both riders should wait for the landing area to clear before beginning the next race.

Riders must slide feet-first while seated or lying on their backs. Head-first sliding, standing, flipping, rough play, jumping from the platform and climbing on exterior walls are prohibited.

A responsible adult must supervise the water slide continuously. Shoes, jewelry, glasses, food, drinks, sharp objects, face paint, confetti and silly string are not permitted on the inflatable.

Setup Requirements

The setup location must be reasonably level, clean and free of rocks, sticks, branches, pet waste, sprinkler heads, irrigation equipment, underground hazards and overhead obstructions.

Because this is a large dual-lane unit, additional space is required around the inflatable for safety stakes, anchoring equipment, blower placement, water connections and participant access.

Important: Tell us whether the setup surface is grass, concrete, asphalt or artificial turf before booking. Hard-surface installations require advance approval and may require sandbags or other approved anchoring equipment.

Electrical Requirements

The Wild Rapid Water Slide requires one suitable electrical outlet for its commercial blower. The outlet should not share a circuit with major appliances or another inflatable blower.

Generator rentals are available for parks, fields and event locations where suitable electricity is unavailable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before reserving the Wild Rapid Water Slide.

How big is the Wild Rapid Water Slide?

It measures approximately 32 feet long, 16 feet wide and 18 feet high. Additional clearance is required for anchoring, blower placement and participant access.

How many riders can slide at once?

The Wild Rapid has two lanes, allowing one rider in each lane at the same time.

Does the customer provide the water?

Yes. The customer must provide a working outdoor water connection and a hose long enough to reach the setup location.

Is setup and takedown included?

Yes. Our team delivers, installs, anchors and later removes the water slide. Delivery charges may vary depending on location.

Does the Wild Rapid require electricity?

Yes. One suitable electrical outlet is required for the blower. Generator rentals are available when electricity is unavailable.

Can it be set up on concrete or asphalt?

Hard-surface setup may be possible with advance approval. Sandbags or other approved anchoring equipment may be required.

Can adults use the water slide?

Use depends on the manufacturer’s height and weight limits. All riders must follow the posted operating and safety rules.

What happens if bad weather is expected?

Setup and operation depend on wind, rain, lightning and other safety conditions. Contact Sen Party Rentals at 571-719-9575 when severe weather is expected.
