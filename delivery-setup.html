Delivery preparation checklist

Remove furniture, toys, branches, rocks, trash and other objects.

Move Vehicles

Approved installation surfaces

Tell us what surface you have

The setup surface affects anchoring, equipment eligibility and pricing.

Preferred

Grass

Grass is normally preferred because inflatables can be secured with stakes.

  • Must be reasonably level
  • Must be free from debris
  • Must allow safe staking

Concrete or Asphalt

Hard-surface setups require approval and additional anchoring equipment.

  • Additional charges may apply
  • Sandbags may be required
  • Not every unit qualifies

Indoor Flooring

Indoor setups depend on ceiling height, floor space, access and anchoring.

  • Measure ceiling height
  • Confirm door clearance
  • Obtain venue approval

Artificial Turf

Artificial turf requires advance review because stakes may not be allowed.

  • Property approval may be required
  • Special anchoring may be needed
  • Additional fees may apply
Equipment access

Make sure we can reach the setup area

Inflatables are large and heavy when rolled. Our team needs a clear and safe path from the delivery vehicle to the installation area.

  • Wide and unlocked gates
  • Clear driveway or loading area
  • No vehicles blocking the path
  • No sharp turns or narrow passages
  • No unsafe mud or loose terrain
  • Advance notice of hills or stairs
  • Permission to enter gated properties

Difficult access may cost more

Additional labor charges may apply when equipment must be moved over long distances, stairs, steep hills, elevators or difficult terrain.

Some locations may be refused when access or setup conditions are unsafe.

Electrical requirements

Power for inflatables

Each inflatable blower requires reliable electrical power throughout the rental.

  • A working outdoor outlet
  • An approved distance from the equipment
  • A dedicated circuit when required
  • A generator for parks or remote areas

Water requirements

Water for slides and dunk tanks

Customers are generally responsible for providing the water source.

  • A working outdoor faucet
  • A hose that reaches the equipment
  • Adequate water pressure
  • Permission to use the venue’s water

Public parks and venues

Planning a park event?

Parks, schools and event venues may have special delivery, permit, insurance, anchoring and electrical requirements.

  • Reserve the park or venue
  • Confirm that inflatables are permitted
  • Obtain required permits
  • Ask about insurance requirements
  • Confirm vehicle access and parking
  • Determine whether a generator is needed
  • Confirm setup and pickup restrictions
After the event

Preparing for pickup

Keep the equipment accessible until our team returns to complete the pickup.

Remove all participants from the equipment.
Remove personal items, food and decorations.
Do not deflate, disconnect or move the rental.
Keep gates and access paths unlocked.
Protect the equipment until it is collected.

Pickup time may vary

Pickup may occur shortly after the event or later in the evening according to the route.

Overnight pickup is available only when approved in advance.
