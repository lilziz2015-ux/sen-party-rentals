Before delivery

Dunk Tank Setup Requirements

Please make sure the event location can safely support the dunk tank before booking.

Water Access

A working outdoor faucet must be available near the setup area so the tank can be filled.

Clear Setup Space

The area must be level, clear and large enough for the tank, target and throwing line.

Easy Delivery Access

The delivery path must be wide enough for the dunk tank and free of stairs, tight turns and obstacles.

Level Surface

The dunk tank must be installed on a stable, level surface such as grass, pavement or concrete.

Drainage Area

The customer must confirm that the tank can be drained safely after the event without causing damage.

Adult Supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the dunk tank throughout the entire event.

Use it safely

Dunk Tank Safety Rules

Follow these rules to help keep participants and guests safe.

01

One Person at a Time

Only one participant may sit on the dunk tank seat at a time.

02

Approved Participants Only

Participants must meet the dunk tank’s height, weight and mobility requirements.

03

No Diving or Jumping

Participants must enter only from the seat. Diving, climbing and jumping into the tank are prohibited.

04

Keep the Area Clear

Guests must remain outside the tank area and behind the marked throwing line.

05

Stop During Unsafe Weather

Do not use the dunk tank during lightning, thunderstorms, high winds or severe weather.

06

Use Approved Throwing Balls

Only approved softballs or supplied balls should be used to activate the target.
